# Privacy

This preview does not enable live support. The intended launcher contacts only the Techieism support website and fixed redemption endpoint when the customer explicitly requests it. It transmits the separately issued one-time support handoff in an HTTPS POST body. It does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, screenshots, passwords or email OTPs itself. The network peer necessarily receives connection metadata such as IP address. No launcher telemetry or analytics is implemented; codes and response bodies are not logged by the launcher.

The website collects customer contact/issue information, CAPTCHA verification and terms/privacy acceptance before email verification. Its published notice governs that separate service: https://help.techieism.com/support-api/privacy . Current registration retention is30days; minimal acceptance evidence is365days. This repository contains none of those records.

The downloaded MeshCentral Assistant connects to Techieism's MeshCentral service and can expose screen, keyboard/mouse control, terminal and files subject to the support permissions and customer consent. Customers should close unrelated sensitive applications and never disclose OTPs or banking/payment credentials. These are material capabilities, not analytics. The operator must display the applicable service privacy policy and obtain the required consent before releasing any handoff code.

Windows signature validation may contact certificate/Windows trust services. Opening the support website invokes the default browser and the website's disclosed services, including Cloudflare Turnstile and email verification. No customer data is submitted to SignPath through launcher operation.

Cancellation before starting support makes no redemption call. A customer can close the launcher or Assistant. End Support revokes authorization on the server. Local temporary payload files are removed on normal exit where possible; crash cleanup is described in README.

Contact: helpdesk@techieism.com.
